# Public discourse ledger (to 2026-09-28)

Compiled from web search and page retrieval on 2026-09-28 to answer: what are people saying about Patoshi, and what have they already tried? Items are recorded as the sources state them. Tags: **[P]** primary evidence or data, **[A]** analysis, **[S]** speculation, **[UNVERIFIED]** not confirmed beyond secondary coverage. Nothing here was reproduced unless the [Phase 4 report](REPORT.md) says so. Sources already reviewed in Phase 3 are in [SOURCES.md](../phase3/SOURCES.md) and are only mentioned here when they have new relevance.

## 1. Critiques and skeptics of the Patoshi attribution

- **Adam Back, X post + coverage (2026-04-26)**. https://twitter.com/adam3us/status/2048287340930437543 ; coverage: https://beincrypto.com/adam-back-satoshi-documentary-bitcoin-theory-flaws/ (Phil Haunhorst), https://yellow.com/news/adam-back-demolishes-satoshi-documentary (Alexey Bondarev). Back wrote that "clearly there were many other miners (60-80% of hashrate or more even in the first year)", that the pattern gets "lost in the noise floor" over time, and that "if Satoshi sold any, he could have sold from more recent, more ambiguous coins first." He does not dispute that Patoshi exists; he disputes that it proves the full holdings or "never sold." [A/S]
- **Szpili/patoshi-forensics, GitHub (created 2026-08-28)**. https://github.com/Szpili/patoshi-forensics . The strongest methodological critique found. Claims: the nonce-LSB classifier has a hard false-positive floor of 19.53% (any miner lands in the 0-9/19-58 band ~1 in 5 times by chance); "the nonce LSB is a signature of software and hardware, not of a person"; the pattern reaches block 0 (p=3.1e-9); Patoshi share declined monotonically (0.85 Jan 2009, 0.70-0.75 spring/summer, 0.39 late Sept, 0.19 Dec) at -0.427%/day, then stopped abruptly within ~500 blocks around May 2010; at blocks 40,000-44,999 an unbiased estimator gives 14.1% Patoshi share vs 1.9% from the extraNonce-chain classifier (undercount late); the standard method produced "33 non-existent mining gaps out of 68" including a spurious 141-hour Easter pause during which Patoshi mined ~78 blocks; found no diurnal rhythm (p=0.99), no timezone in pauses, no correlation with external events. Single anonymous author, zero stars, not peer reviewed. [A, UNVERIFIED reproduction]
- **Cointelegraph Magazine "Big Questions" (2026-09-22, Christina Comben)**. https://cointelegraph.com/magazine/big-questions-does-satoshi-actually-own-1-million-bitcoin . Quotes Bitquery's Gaurav Agrawal: strict application of the fingerprint covers "just under 0.9 million BTC", most generous ~1.17M; the one-machine claim is strong, the machine-was-Satoshi claim is circumstantial, and current key control is unprovable ("the chain only records that someone had it"). [A]
- **Older critiques still cited**: BitMEX Research 2018 (0.6-0.7M BTC, questioned nonce-pattern reliability; already known). Lerner's 2019 "Return of the Deniers" rebutted the "many synchronized miners" hypothesis (known). Whale Alert (2020-07-20, https://whale-alert.medium.com/the-satoshi-fortune-e49cf73f9a9b ; https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2020/07/20/whale-alert-identifies-1125-million-btc-as-satoshis-stash): 22,503 of first 54,316 blocks, 1,125,150 BTC, and argued Satoshi also mined some non-Patoshi blocks (pushes the other direction). [A]
- **btc-rpc-explorer discussion 465** (known): Lerner conceded some blocks can be slope-finder false positives; the thread discusses a >=300 s spacing rule in connection with block 24504. Relevant as a published false-positive admission. Note: 24504 *is* in the pinned Lopp list used here, 65 s after listed block 24503; 143 of 21,952 consecutive listed pairs are under 300 s, so the rule was not applied as a hard filter. Phase 4 independently finds 24504 co-spent with 38 unlisted coinbases. [A]
- **Pattern after block ~50,000 / stop date**: Lopp list ends at 49,973; Whale Alert and satoshi-onchain extend to ~54,316-54,458; Szpili claims a residual ~14% share at 40k-45k that the chain classifier misses. A Sept 2026 X thread (via KuCoin community repost, https://www.kucoin.com/news/community/BTC/6ab38a0d74fd460007c4f068 , author "Qt") claims Satoshi also mined non-Patoshi blocks until 2009-12-24, paused one month, and returned intermittently in 2010. [S, UNVERIFIED]
- **No formal critique found** from Chainalysis, Coin Metrics or Glassnode specifically on Patoshi. Arkham and Galaxy adopt the pattern (see section 3).

## 3. On-chain labels and "Satoshi coins moved" false alarms

- **Arkham** (2025-02-04) https://info.arkm.com/announcements/update-100-billion-in-satoshi-nakamotos-holdings-now-on-arkham . Added ~22,000 Patoshi-pattern addresses (1,096,354 BTC) to its "Satoshi Nakamoto" entity, including the only known addresses Satoshi spent from. It does not say which list it used. [P label / A]
- **Galaxy Research (Alex Thorn)** uses "profiles for Satoshi clusters" to rule wallets in or out (2026 coverage).
- **False alarms / debunks**:
  - 2024-01-05: 26.9 BTC sent to the genesis address from a fresh Binance-funded wallet; genesis coinbase is unspendable, so no signal. https://cointelegraph.com/news/crypto-community-bitcoin-transfer-satoshi-genesis-block [P]
  - 2025-07-04 to 07-25: 80,000 BTC whale (dormant since Apr 2011) sold through Galaxy; CryptoQuant traced its origin to MyBitcoin; not Patoshi. https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/07/25/bitcoin-rebounds-after-galaxy-completes-sale-of-usd9b-btc-from-satoshi-era-whale [P]
  - 2025-07-31: 250 BTC from five wallets mined 2010-04-26; analysts ruled out Patoshi by nonce range; Whale Alert added that "at least one of the non-Patoshi patterns belongs to Satoshi as well" [S]. https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560604889063
  - 2026-05-31: 20 BTC from 1CDSyXAQxro4FPUoqAQb81642ruqDsUiNp (dormant since Aug 2010); Galaxy said it did not match Satoshi clusters. https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/satoshi-era-bitcoin-wallet-moves-20-btc-after-15-8-years
  - 2026-09-05: 600 BTC from twelve March-2010 mining addresses; Whale Alert said not Satoshi. https://shattered.io/bitcoin-wallet-moves-600-btc-16-year-dormancy-2026/ [UNVERIFIED source quality]
- **"Noah Doe" lost-property lawsuit (NEW, major)**. *ABC Company, XYZ Company and Noah Doe v. John Does 1-39,069*, NY Supreme Court Index No. 153119/2026, filed 2026-03-11, amended 2026-05-01. It claims 39,069 dormant addresses (~3.8M BTC) as abandoned property, including ~21,923 Patoshi addresses (Galaxy count). Notices were served on-chain via OP_RETURN dust by "Salomon Brothers Strategic Advisors" in 2025. Galaxy/Thorn: 44 addresses were dropped after they moved, and "there's no evidence any of the 39K addresses are 'lost'". The case was stayed after a July 14 hearing; a Sept 8 hearing on intervention motions (Bitcoin Policy Institute, Digital Chamber) had an unknown outcome in the sources found; amicus briefs were filed. Sources: https://cryptoslate.com/new-lawsuit-claims-satoshi-nakamotos-bitcoin-is-lost-property-worth-under-10-per-wallet/ (2026-05-29), https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/06/06/satoshi-era-bitcoin-at-center-of-usd285-billion-lawsuit-moves-after-14-years , https://u.today/satoshi-ownership-lawsuit-fails (2026-07-08), https://www.tftc.io/dormant-bitcoin-161m-noah-doe-lawsuit-wallet-movements (2026-09-22: 1,971 BTC moved from four wallets, three of them named in the suit; none reported as Patoshi). One report gives dust block heights 950,446-950,576 "in June-July 2025", which does not match 2025 chain height. [P court filings; UNVERIFIED details]

## 4. Quantum debate and Patoshi (P2PK) coins

Status in bitcoin/bips README (fetched 2026-09-28):
- **BIP-360 "Pay-to-Merkle-Root (P2MR)"**, authors Hunter Beast, Ethan Heilman, Isabel Foxen Duke. Specification, **Draft**, merged 2026-02-11. Taproot-like SegWit v2 output (bc1z) with the key path removed. It protects only new outputs and does nothing for existing P2PK coins. It was earlier named P2QRH and then P2TSH (naming history from memory, not re-verified). Not activated; BTQ Technologies runs it on a separate testnet (2026-03). https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0360.mediawiki
- **BIP-361 "Post Quantum Migration and Legacy Signature Sunset"**, authors Lopp, Papathanasiou, Smith, Ross, Vaile, Dallaire-Demers. Informational, **Draft** (number assigned 2026-02-11, published April 2026). Phase A (~3 years) bans sends to vulnerable outputs; Phase B (~5 years) invalidates ECDSA/Schnorr spends, which would freeze un-migrated P2PK coins including Patoshi. It adds an optional ZK proof of BIP-39 seed possession for recovery, which cannot help Patoshi's pre-BIP-39 keys. https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/pull/1895 , https://bips.dev/361/ [P]
- **Hourglass (V1 2025, V2 posted 2026-02-10)**, Hunter Beast / cryptoquick fork, no BIP number. Limits P2PK spends to one per block, and V2 also caps them at 1 BTC per block, so draining P2PK would take 32+ years. Objections on bitcoindev from Lopp, Isabel Foxen Duke, Brandon Black and others. https://groups.google.com/g/bitcoindev/c/0E1UyyQIUA0 , https://github.com/cryptoquick/bips/blob/hourglass-v2/bip-hourglass-v2.mediawiki
- **Other proposals**: Tadge Dryja's commit/reveal soft fork idea; SLH-DSA/SHRINCS signatures (CoinDesk 2026-04-04 roundup, https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/04/04/bitcoin-s-usd1-3-trillion-security-race-key-initiatives-aimed-at-quantum-proofing-the-world-s-largest-blockchain ). Dan Robinson's (Paradigm) **PACTs** (2026-05-01, https://www.paradigm.xyz/2026/05/pacts-protecting-your-bitcoin-from-a-quantum-sunset ) let holders privately timestamp BIP-322 proofs now and later unlock with STARKs after a sunset. Nic Carter proposed a legal trust for Satoshi's coins.
- **Threat estimates**: Chaincode Labs (Milton and Shikhelman, May 2025, https://chaincode.com/bitcoin-post-quantum.pdf ) puts ~1.6-1.7M BTC in P2PK and 20-50% of supply as vulnerable. CoinShares (2026-02-06) argues only ~10,200 BTC sit in P2PK outputs big enough to move markets. Google Quantum AI whitepaper (2026-03-30/31, https://quantumai.google/static/site-assets/downloads/cryptocurrency-whitepaper.pdf ) estimates <500k physical qubits and ~9 min per key, with ~6.9M BTC having exposed pubkeys. [A]
- **Freeze vs. let-steal debate**: CoinDesk 2026-07-04 https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/04/bitcoin-experts-split-over-plan-to-freeze-satoshi-s-1-1-million-bitcoin-as-quantum-threat-grows . CZ floated freezing unmoved Satoshi coins; Lopp prefers freezing to theft; Michael Terpin calls it a "slippery slope"; Matt Hougan backs Carter's trust idea. No consensus and no activation client exists. [A/S]

## 5. Identity claims that touch Patoshi / on-chain data

- **NYT, John Carreyrou (2026-04-08)** names Adam Back as the likeliest Satoshi. The evidence is stylometric (Back matches 67 of 325 hyphenation quirks, next-best suspect 38; Florian Cafiero stylometry), plus mailing-list silence and the 2008 emails. Critics note it uses no on-chain or Patoshi evidence and no key signature (Brave New Coin, 2026-04-10, https://bravenewcoin.com/insights/new-york-times-names-adam-back-as-bitcoins-creator-but-the-evidence-falls-short ). Back denies it. [A/S]
- **"Finding Satoshi" film (released 2026-04-22; William D. Cohan and Tyler Maroney)** names Hal Finney and Len Sassaman. It leans on Patoshi (500k-1M BTC) and on the argument that the coins never moved, so Satoshi is dead. Rebuttals: Back's post (section 1); Sassaman's widow says he knew no C++ and owned no Windows machine; Lopp's Finney alibi. [S]
- **Lopp, "Hal Finney Was Not Satoshi Nakamoto" (2024-10-21)** https://blog.lopp.net/hal-finney-was-not-satoshi-nakamoto/ . Finney was running a 10-mile race on 2009-04-18 while Satoshi's transaction to Mike Hearn was broadcast (between blocks at 8:28 and ~8:55 PT). This is on-chain timing evidence. [A]
- **HBO "Money Electric" (2024-10-08, Peter Todd)**: forum-post based, no Patoshi evidence; Back rejected a renewed Todd theory on 2026-06-18. **COPA v Wright** (judgment March 2024): Wright is not Satoshi and never signed with early keys. Neither relies on Patoshi. Bitcoin.com (2026-05-30) summary: https://news.bitcoin.com/todd-back-sassaman-and-finney-named-satoshi-in-3-investigations-that-found-no-proof/
- **Peter Miller, "What time zone was Satoshi Nakamoto in?" (Medium, 2026-06-10)** https://medium.com/@tgof137/satoshi-nakamoto-time-zone-and-timeline-90edfcacefd0 . Uses the bensig Patoshi CSV to match mining gaps to conferences: DEFCON 17 (Jul 29-Aug 3 2009), CRYPTO 2009 (Aug 14-24), Toorcon (Oct 23-24), Black Hat DC (Jan 30-Feb 2 2010), Hackito Ergo Sum (Apr 7-9 2010). Concludes US time zone, not Japan; admits cherry-picking risk. This conflicts with Szpili's finding of no timezone signal in pauses. [S]

## Research gaps / open ideas mentioned publicly

1. Reconcile block/address counts (21,953 vs 21,923 vs 21,744 vs ~22,500) with a published diff and explicit inclusion rules (spent coinbases, duplicate keys, 49,973 vs ~54,400 cutoff).
2. Quantify the classifier false-positive rate (Szpili's 19.53% floor) and publish per-block posterior probabilities instead of a binary list (satoshi-onchain's φ score is one attempt).
3. Estimate the late-period Patoshi share (blocks 40k-55k) with an unbiased estimator; test "gradual throttling vs abrupt stop."
4. Separate real mining gaps from classifier artifacts, then test gap and conference/travel alignment against a null model (Miller vs Szpili disagree).
5. Look for a diurnal/sleep signal in Patoshi timestamps (Lopp 2022 says Pacific; Szpili finds none), controlling for the >=300 s timestamp rule and clock skew.
6. Check whether Satoshi also mined non-Patoshi blocks (Whale Alert 2020, 2026 X claims), e.g. with the stock single-thread client on a second machine.
7. Correlate mining pauses with Satoshi's forum/email/SVN timestamps (Miller does this partly with the Jan 2009 Hal emails).
8. Test the double-helix / two-instance and 25-hour-gap reorg hypotheses (Wicked 2024) against orphan-block archives, if any exist.
9. Build a reproducible "is this Patoshi?" verdict tool for dormant-wallet movements to cut false alarms (satoshi-onchain `judge.py`).
10. Map the Patoshi addresses against the Noah Doe defendant list and the eCash reassignment set to document exactly which lists legal and fork actors rely on.
11. Quantum: Patoshi coinbases pay to bare P2PK, so every pubkey is already exposed. Open question: how BIP-361 Phase B, Hourglass V2 (1 BTC/block, i.e. ~22,000 x 50 BTC outputs) or PACTs would treat them. No source models this per output.
