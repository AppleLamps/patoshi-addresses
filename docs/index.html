How the addresses relate to the chain.

In 2009 block rewards were paid in the original pay-to-public-key (P2PK) format. Each address in the index is the standard Base58 address for that reward, which is what wallets and explorers show. Balances and spend states come from the original reward output, not from later payments to the address. Every record also carries the public key itself.

Attribution is not identity.

“Patoshi” is a mining-pattern classification. It does not cryptographically prove one miner, or that the miner was Satoshi Nakamoto. All 134 additions are labeled separately; twelve published blocks remain marked as contradicted by the audit’s co-spend evidence.
